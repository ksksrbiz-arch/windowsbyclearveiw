# Stage 04 — Installation

## Inputs

`03-materials` output, `02-openings` output, manufacturer instructions, and installation/water-management references.

## Process

Produce a field sequence:

1. verify product/opening;
2. protect site and confirm conditions;
3. inspect opening and stop for concealed damage;
4. establish appropriate sill/drainage and flashing transitions;
5. set and support the unit;
6. fasten only to the exact product instructions;
7. integrate exterior flashing/WRB transitions;
8. air-seal and insulate appropriate gaps;
9. trim/finish;
10. verify drainage, operation, weather seals, and finish.

The sequence is general unless exact manufacturer instructions establish a more specific method.

## Outputs

`installation.md` with project sequence, opening-specific deviations, VERIFY gates, and source list.

## Stop conditions

No applicable manufacturer instructions for a product where they matter; unresolved concealed damage; or a proposed step depends on an invented specification.
