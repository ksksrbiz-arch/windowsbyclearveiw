Woodland is the north edge of the usual drive: Cowlitz County, still close enough for a Vancouver crew. Incorporated in 1906, it sits where the Lewis River meets the Columbia and has a real flood history — the town is diked for it.

## Housing in Woodland

That flood history is part of why the older housing stock here is what it is — established, river-town construction rather than sprawling new subdivisions. The newest builds are a small share of what's actually in Woodland.

## What tends to go wrong

Replacement is the typical job rather than new construction — older sash and early-generation vinyl reaching the end of a normal service life, the same failure points as anywhere else in the region, just on an older average house.
