La Center is a short drive north of Vancouver, on the East Fork Lewis River. It is a small, tight-knit town — incorporated in 1909, with a bit over 4,000 residents — so most jobs are word of mouth as much as a phone call.

## Housing in La Center

A small town this size doesn't have one dominant building era — older in-town houses close to the river sit alongside newer acreage homes on the outskirts, without the large subdivisions that define growth in places like Ridgefield or Battle Ground.

## What tends to go wrong

Same rain as the rest of Clark County, same tired sliders and fogged insulated units — a cloudy pane that never wipes clean is a failed seal, not a cleaning problem (see [fogged windows](/guides/fogged-windows)). On the older river-adjacent houses, original wood sash and drafty frames show up more often than in the newer acreage builds.

## What we do here

We measure, order, and install from Vancouver — insert or full-frame depending on what the opening actually needs. If the house is new construction, we can set nailing-fin units once the openings are ready; see [new construction](/new-construction) for what we ask a builder to have in hand first.
