La Center is a short drive north of Vancouver, on the East Fork Lewis River. It is a small, tight-knit town — incorporated in 1909, with a bit over 4,000 residents — so most jobs are word of mouth as much as a phone call. Same rain as the rest of Clark County, same tired sliders and fogged insulated units.

We measure, order, and install from Vancouver. If the house is new construction, we can set nailing-fin units once the openings are ready.

Nearby: [Ridgefield](/areas/ridgefield) and [Vancouver](/areas/vancouver).
