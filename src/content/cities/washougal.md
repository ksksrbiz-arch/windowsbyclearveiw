Washougal gets Columbia River Gorge weather along SR-14: wind and a long wet season that finds a weak spot in a wall faster than a sheltered lot would. The town has an older riverside core, with newer growth spreading east and up the hill.

## Housing in Washougal

The older riverside blocks have original sashes and early vinyl replacements that are due for another round. The newer construction east and up the hill is recent enough that most of it is sound, standard vinyl.

## What tends to go wrong

In the riverside core, the two most common complaints are sashes that stick and single-pane rooms that stay cold. In the newer east-county builds, the more common issue is wind-driven rain getting in at a poorly sealed slider track or a flashing lap, rather than the glass itself.

## What we do here

We drive from Vancouver, measure each opening, and install replacement or new-construction windows suited to this climate. See [vinyl vs. fiberglass in the Pacific Northwest](/guides/vinyl-vs-fiberglass-pacific-northwest) for which material holds up against Gorge wind on a wide opening.
