Brush Prairie runs to acreage, older ranches, and pole-adjacent builds that went up before anyone worried much about U-factor. A lot of what we pull out here is original aluminum or first-generation vinyl with failed seals.

Openings on rural lots take more wind and more driven rain than a tucked-in city street, so the flashing detail matters more than the sticker on the glass. We measure each opening for insert or full-frame, set the unit plumb, and seal it for weather that comes in sideways.

New construction is common out here too. If you are a builder framing off 117th, 182nd, or out toward Hockinson, we set nailing-fin units on a scheduled date so your siding crew is not waiting on us.
