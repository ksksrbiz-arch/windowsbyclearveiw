Ridgefield is one of the fastest-growing cities in Washington right now, and it shows in the openings we measure.

## Housing in Ridgefield

That growth means a small historic downtown surrounded by a lot of new framed openings. New subdivisions are filling in faster than almost anywhere else in the county, and older in-town housing is a small share of what is here now.

## What tends to go wrong

In the historic downtown, it is the usual older-house problems: original sashes, drafts, and a frame that has settled out of square. In the new subdivisions, real defects are rare because most of the housing is only a few years old. When something is wrong, it is more often a builder-grade window or an installation detail than the window itself.

## What we do here

We do both: replacement in existing walls, and windows set while the house is still in wrap. Homeowner? We walk the openings with you. Builder? Bring the plan set and the window schedule, and see [new construction](/new-construction) for exactly what we check against it before ordering.
