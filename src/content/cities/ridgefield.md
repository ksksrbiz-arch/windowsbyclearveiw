Ridgefield is growing fast — one of the fastest-growing cities in Washington right now, and it shows in the openings we measure.

## Housing in Ridgefield

That growth means a small historic downtown surrounded by a lot of new framed openings. New subdivisions are filling in faster than almost anywhere else in the county, and older in-town housing is a small share of what's actually here now.

## What tends to go wrong

In the historic downtown pocket, it's the usual older-house problems — original sash, drafts, a frame settled out of square. In the new subdivisions, actual defects are rare since most of the stock is only a few years old. When something's wrong, it's more often a builder-grade unit or an install detail than the window itself.

## What we do here

We do both — pull-and-replace in existing walls, and nailing-fin sets when the house is still in wrap. Homeowner? We walk the openings. Builder? Bring the plan set and the window schedule; see [new construction](/new-construction) for exactly what we check against it before ordering.
