Ridgefield incorporated in 1909 on the Columbia River, next to the Ridgefield National Wildlife Refuge and Lake River. It is growing fast now — population is up roughly 50% since 2020, and it gets cited as one of the fastest-growing cities in Washington.

## Housing in Ridgefield

That growth rate means a small historic downtown surrounded by a lot of new framed openings — new subdivisions filling in faster than almost anywhere else in the county, with the older in-town housing stock a small fraction of what's actually here now.

## What tends to go wrong

In the historic downtown pocket, it's the usual older-house problems — original sash, drafts, a frame that's settled out of square. In the new subdivisions, actual defects are rare since most of the stock is only a few years old; when something is wrong, it's more often a builder-grade unit or an install detail than the window itself.

## What we do here

We do both — pull-and-replace in existing walls, and nailing-fin sets when the house is still in wrap. If you're the homeowner, we walk the openings.
