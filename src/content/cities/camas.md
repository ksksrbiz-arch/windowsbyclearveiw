Camas houses sit in the same marine climate as Vancouver, with a mix of older downtown stock and newer builds toward Lacamas Lake. Camas incorporated in 1906 as a paper-mill town, and that older core still has original single-pane sash mixed in with whatever got swapped in over the decades. The city has grown past 27,000 residents, most of that growth in the newer subdivisions north and east of downtown rather than the old mill core. North Camas is the opposite problem from downtown: recent subdivisions, mostly good windows, occasional builder-grade units worth a second look.

We replace tired units and set new-construction windows when a house is going up. A Camas job is a Vancouver-based crew, a written estimate after we see the openings, and the same flashing and finish standards as the rest of Clark County.
